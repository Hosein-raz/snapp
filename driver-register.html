FAQ

How many people can register as drivers with one car on Snapp?

A maximum of 3 people can register and operate with one car on Snapp Drivers.

How many days after registering my account will it be activated?

We will inform you via SMS about your registration status between one and three days.

Can I register drivers for any car model on Snapp?

Unfortunately, this model of cars does not allow drivers to register on Snapp:
Renault PK
Paykan

How can I track my registration status?

You can track your registration status by clicking on the "Register" or "Follow" button at the top of this page.

What is the minimum age to register as a Snapp driver?

To register as a Snapp driver, you must be at least 18 years old.

Do I need a "no criminal record" certificate to register as a Snapp driver?

After registration, Snapp's security team verifies the background check with the police to ensure there is no criminal record.
