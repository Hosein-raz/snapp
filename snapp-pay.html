FAQ

What is a credit service?

The credit service is a quick and easy way to pay for expenses without having a balance. By activating this service, you will be given a certain amount of credit until the end of the same month and you can use your credit to pay for travel, order food, order from the supermarket and request Snapp Doctor services without having a balance. Please note that you have the opportunity to settle your bill from the date of use until the end of each month.

How can I activate my credit service?

The credit service is only activated for selected users who are eligible. To activate this feature, click on the "Credit Service" icon on the Super App home page and then register for this service for free by entering your national ID number. If you are eligible, the credit service will be activated for you.

How can I deactivate my credit service?

Currently, it is not possible to deactivate the credit service in the application, but if you do not use the credit service to pay for your own expenses, you will not be billed.

If I am unable to use all of my credit service balance by the end of the period, what happens to the remaining balance?

You have the opportunity to use your credit service balance until the end of the specified period. Please note that once the period ends, the remaining balance cannot be transferred to the next month.

What should I do if my Snapp related fees exceed my credit service balance?

In this case, you cannot use the credit service to pay for your expenses, but you can choose another payment method.

What happens if I don't pay my bill on time?

If you do not pay your bill on time, in addition to the fact that you will no longer be able to use your credit service, an amount equivalent to five percent of the unpaid debt will be calculated as a late payment penalty and added to your bill amount, and for each month of delay, another penalty amount will be recalculated and added to the previous month's penalty. Over time, if you do not pay your debt, your information will be provided to regulatory and credit rating agencies as a bad customer.

Can I get more credit?

You can get new credit for that month by paying your bill at any time.
