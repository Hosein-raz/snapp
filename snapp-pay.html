Benefits of the Snapp! Credit Service

  • Pay in the future

    By activating the Snapp credit service, you can use credit to pay for travel, food orders, goods and other services without worrying about immediate payment, and pay the consumption cost at the end of the month or in installments depending on the type of purchase.

  • No need for a guarantor or fee

    An important feature of Snapp's credit service is the lack of need for collateral, guarantors, or fees.

  • Fast and reliable delivery

    One of the strengths of Snapp Credit is the fast and reliable delivery of purchased products. In some cities, orders even reach buyers in under two hours!
